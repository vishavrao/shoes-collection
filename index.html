GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions.

Can I order through this demo?

The cart is a preview only. Checkout, payments and order processing need to be connected before launch.

Are sizes and prices available?

Sizes, prices and stock details have not been provided yet. Product pages can be completed once those details are confirmed.

How do I contact the store?

The contact form shows the intended experience. A business email or contact service needs to be connected before real enquiries can be received.

What are the shipping and returns terms?

Final shipping, returns and refund terms need approval from the business before the store goes live.

CLEAR INFORMATION

Policies & information.

Privacy

This demo keeps saved items, bag selections and sample account details in this browser’s local storage. No order or contact message is sent to a server. A final privacy policy must identify the business, data collected, processors, retention, rights and contact details.

Terms of use

Product names and photos are for demonstration. Prices, availability and descriptions require confirmation. The live store needs final terms covering orders, payment, cancellations and customer support.

Shipping & returns

Shipping destinations, costs, delivery estimates, return window, item condition requirements and refund process will be published after the business approves them.

Accessibility

The demo supports keyboard navigation, readable labels and mobile layouts. Feedback on accessibility can be sent using the future live contact channel.
